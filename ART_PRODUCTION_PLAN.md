# Arcane Survivor — Art Production Plan

Stage B.2 · plan only. **Do not begin any step below without explicit production authorization.** Each component and its variants must be reviewed individually before final approval. No new images, exports, frames or preview tools were created for this plan.

## Approval workflow

Before production: settle the four blocking questions in [CHARACTER_SYSTEM.md](CHARACTER_SYSTEM.md), then approve a bounded component brief. Produce only that component. Review its isolated transparent export, nearest-enlarged view, registered overlays, all four frame poses, and a gameplay-scale composite against the existing environment. Those are proposed future review artifacts, not screenshots already produced.

After visual review, test a separately approved preview integration on iPhone: stable feet, no clipping, clean borders, correct proportions and readable details. Component approval should identify file/revision/checksum, approved palette/poses and any limitations. Technical compatibility and final-art approval are separate gates. Approval of a hairstyle does not approve every hat pairing; approval of a color variant does not approve a new outfit silhouette. Keep the last accepted preview until replacement passes.

## Quality checks common to every component

- Original references untouched; editable source and reproducible export instructions retained.
- Document logical dimensions separately from physical PNG dimensions; correct alpha, registration, frame ordering and palette ownership.
- Test no-outfit/coverage, short/long hair and weapon proximity as dependencies become available. Do not require unproduced pieces merely to finish an earlier step.
- Shared anatomy must survive presentation, color and outfit swaps. Check hands without hair/cape hiding the join.
- No unintended filtering blur, matte borders, sprite clipping, frame jitter or foot drift at normal gameplay scale.
- Do not infer licensing/provenance from a filename. Confirm rights/provenance before release; no rights audit is claimed here.
- Record the owner's actual approval; unresolved/rejected variants stay review-only. Keep versioned prior approved components for rollback.

## Scope and scheduling

This is a dependency order, not a calendar or effort promise. Body approval is the critical dependency. Eye/hair iterations should not finalize before the head template is approved; color variants follow silhouette approval. Weapon and hat reviews must not delay unrelated completed approvals. No production, gameplay, monetization, save/UI implementation or deployment is authorized by this plan. Stop at B.2.
